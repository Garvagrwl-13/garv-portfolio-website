Leadership & Sanskrit Community.

Building spaces where Sanskrit is practiced, discussed, and shared.

Community Vivakshu वाग्वर्धिनी
Community work

Language becomes alive in company.

Alongside research, my work with Sanskrit has centered on creating spaces where people can speak, debate, and take part in the language together.

Two initiatives

One encourages practice through conversation; the other established a platform for debate and student leadership.

01

Vivakshu.

A platform for Sanskrit enthusiasts and conversation practice.

Speak it. Use it.

Co-founder · 2023–Present

Vivakshu is a platform for Sanskrit enthusiasts focused on encouraging conversational Sanskrit and making active use of the language more natural and accessible.

Its work is practice-oriented: participants meet online to converse in Sanskrit, bringing learners together from India and abroad.
