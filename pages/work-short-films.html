→ Director's note

The frame is a śloka. The cut is its caesura.

I think of film the way I think of a verse — as a meter you can fall into. Every script is an exercise in seeing what Sanskrit already knew about timing: when to hold a syllable, when to let it go, when one breath ends and the next begins.

The work isn't always about the language. But the language is always the spine — even when the dialogue is in Hindi, even when there is no dialogue at all. Especially then.
