# 6. Design Principles

The Work section should feel like a **portfolio**, not a second CV.

Prefer:
- actual work
- your contribution
- concrete numbers
- dates
- links
- screenshots
- certificates
- research materials
- videos

Avoid:
- giant paragraphs
- skill bars
- generic “passionate/hardworking” language
- unsupported superlatives
- excessive certificates
- future projects presented as completed work
- unnecessary technology buzzwords

---

# 7. Evidence Hierarchy

Prioritize:

1. **Actual work**
2. **Your contribution**
3. **Result/output**
4. **External validation**
5. **Supporting material**

The certificates should validate the work, not become the work.
