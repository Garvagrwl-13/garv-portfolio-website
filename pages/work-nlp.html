Pāṇini-aware morphological parser

A transformer-based parser that doesn't just label morphemes — it predicts the Aṣṭādhyāyī rule chain that would have produced them. Sandhi-aware tokenisation, sūtra-conditioned attention, evaluated against the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit.

Sandhi as a seq-to-seq problem

A small character-level encoder–decoder learns the inverse of internal-sandhi: take a fused surface form and emit its underlying word boundary. Compared against rule-based baselines (saMsAdhanI, Sanskrit-Heritage) on five test sets.

Embedding the śloka

A contrastive objective trains verse embeddings on the dual signal of meaning and metre. Same śloka in different metres lands close; same metre with different meaning stays apart. A small but useful primitive for stylometric retrieval.
