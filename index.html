Who I am.

I'm Garv. I work where Sanskrit meets deep learning — teaching the language as a living system, and using modern models to understand its inner structure.

My belief is simple: Sanskrit isn't a relic to be archived. It's an instrument, beautifully engineered, still in tune. The work on this site is one person's attempt to keep it that way — in print, on camera, in code.

Garv Aggarwal — New Delhi
